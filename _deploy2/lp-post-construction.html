Good To Know

Common questions

What does post-construction cleaning include?

Construction dust removed from every surface high and low, sticker and adhesive removal, paint and caulk spotting, inside cabinets and drawers, vents and fixtures, windows and tracks, and a full floor detail. We run it in up to three phases — rough, final and punch-list touch-up — depending on what you need.

What does it cost?

It's priced per project. Square footage, the phases you need and the dust load all change the number, so a published rate would be wrong more often than right. Send the address, square footage and walkthrough date and you'll have a real quote the same day at no cost.

How soon can you mobilize?

Depends on the size of the job and where it sits in our schedule. Call with your closing or walkthrough date and we'll give you a straight yes or no — we'd rather turn down a date than miss one.

Do you work directly with general contractors and builders?

Yes. We work with builders, GCs, remodelers and property management groups across the Houston metro, on single custom homes and on multi-unit communities. Ask about recurring vendor pricing.
