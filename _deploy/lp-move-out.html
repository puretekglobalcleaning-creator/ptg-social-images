Good To Know

Common questions

What does a move-out clean actually include?

A full top-to-bottom clean of an empty unit: appliances inside and out, cabinets and drawers cleaned inside, bathrooms sanitized, baseboards and door frames wiped, light fixtures and vents, windows and tracks, and all floors vacuumed and mopped. It targets exactly what a landlord or property manager checks at inspection.

How much does move-out cleaning cost in Houston?

Ours start at $250 and most land between $250 and $550, based on square footage and condition. You get a flat price up front — not an hourly rate that grows while we work.

How fast can you get here?

Most standard units are same-day or next-day depending on size and our schedule. Call or text with your move-out date and we'll tell you straight whether we can hit it.

Will this get my deposit back?

A thorough move-out clean addresses exactly what landlords inspect for. We can't guarantee a landlord's decision, but we make sure the unit is left in the best possible condition and we'll come back if something was missed.
