Yes. A large part of what we do is commercial and property-management work — office and janitorial cleaning, medical suites, HOA common areas, recurring move-out and turnover cleans, and post-construction cleanup for builders. Call us and we'll build a plan around your volume.

Yes — Puretek Global Cleaning is a licensed, insured LLC. We're happy to provide documentation for commercial and property-management accounts.

Yes. Our promise is simple: it's clean and you're satisfied, or we make it right. If anything isn't up to standard, tell us and we'll come back.
