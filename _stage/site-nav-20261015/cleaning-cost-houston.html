What Drives the Price

What affects the cost of a cleaning in Houston

  • Square footage — the biggest factor for standard home cleaning.
  • Type of clean — a first-time deep clean or move-out costs more than recurring upkeep.
  • Frequency — weekly and biweekly visits are priced lower per visit than one-time cleans.
  • Condition — heavy build-up, post-construction dust, or vacant units take more time.
  • Add-ons — inside oven or fridge ($45 each), interior windows ($12 each), laundry, and baseboards.
  • Commercial scope — offices, medical suites, HOA common areas, and post-construction are quoted per project.
